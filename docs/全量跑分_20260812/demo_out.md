The book distinguishes actus reus from mens rea by defining actus reus as the prohibited act, such as 'an unlawful killing', while mens rea refers to the 'guilty mind' or 'fault element' [p.11]. 

**从功能上看**，actus reus represents the physical act that is prohibited by law, while mens rea refers to the mental state or intention behind the act. For instance, in the case of murder, the prosecution must demonstrate that the act of killing occurred (actus reus) and that it was done with malice aforethought (mens rea) [p.11].
